# Component savings across powertrains

19 October 2026. Follow-up to multisystem engineering research. No workbook changes.

## Finding

BEV-specific study context does not make all mechanisms BEV-specific. Transfer general mechanisms by subsystem; do not transfer the aggregate 10–25% EV material-cost opportunity wholesale to ICE vehicles.

## Independent non-BEV-specific evidence

- [Toyota CCC21 history](https://www.toyota-global.com/company/history_of_toyota/75years/text/leaping_forward_as_a_global_corporation/chapter4/section7/item2.html): July 2000 program targeted 30% cost reductions on 173 selected components. Early supplier involvement, common components across models and integrated modules. Target, not realized whole-vehicle percentage; historical comparator.
- [McKinsey complexity study](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/calculating-complexity-maximizing-the-value-of-customization): automotive instrument-cluster standardization example, 3% savings. Choosing the more expensive hardware reduced broader lifecycle costs. No powertrain restriction stated. Not 3% of the vehicle BOM and not exclusively materials.
- [McKinsey design/material methodology](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/mastering-the-dual-mission-carbon-and-cost-savings): material substitution, design and supplier choices are broader mechanisms; its quantified vehicle example remains EV-specific. Do not relabel that example ICE evidence.

## Transfer assessment (analyst inference)

| Lever | ICE | HEV/PHEV/REEV | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shared seats, trim, fasteners, switches and modules | Applicable | Applicable | Preserve valued differentiation; content deletion may reduce revenue |
| Variant reduction | Applicable | Applicable | Savings cross purchasing, plant, inventory, engineering and warranty; count once |
| ADAS/compute consolidation | Applicable | Applicable | Requires architecture compatibility and incremental redesign |
| Body consolidation and material optimization | Applicable | Applicable | Validate crash, towing, corrosion, repairability; not all structures interchangeable |
| Supplier standardization, requirements review | Applicable | Applicable | Reject over-specification, not required performance |
| Battery chemistry/cell procurement | Not traction-battery lever | Applicable to actual battery subsystem | HEV small power-oriented packs differ from BEV energy-oriented packs |
| Efficiency permitting smaller traction battery | Not same mechanism | Conditional | Hold range, power and durability constant; no linear extrapolation |
| Integrated e-drive/power electronics | Not combustion drivetrain | Applicable where electric equipment exists | Existing integrated equipment is not a new saving |
| Integrated thermal circuits | General simplification possible | Architecture-specific | Engine heat and multiple cooling temperatures change economics |
| Structural battery pack | Not applicable | Limited/platform-specific | Not a generic whole-body saving |

## North American exposure

[Current global strategy](https://www.stellantis.com/en/company/our-strategy) gives 29 BEV, 15 PHEV/REEV, 24 HEV and 39 ICE/MHEV planned powertrain launches through 2030. Counts include multi-energy products, are global, and are not sales shares. Do not divide these counts to estimate North American 2028 BEV mix.

[North American Investor Day transcript](https://www.stellantis.com/content/dam/stellantis-corporate/investors/events/stellantis-investor-day-2026/transcript/Stellantis-Investor-Day-2026-FaSTLAne2030-Transcript-North-America.pdf) outlines ICE, hybrid, range-extended pickup/large SUV and Recon BEV plans. No quantified 2028 NA powertrain sales split was established in this pass. Existing model forecasts are insufficient: Charger, Ram and future products require variant take rates. See the existing launch register and electrified/architectures.json for dated model evidence. Cherokee HEV is distinct from Grand Cherokee; canceled pure-BEV Ram program is distinct from renamed REEV program.

Current global technology adoption guidance: 35% of 2030 annual volume with at least one of Brain, SmartCockpit or AutoDrive. This does not imply 35% on all three, 35% NA, or full adoption by 2028.

## Model treatment

For each non-overlapping project: eligible production units × subsystem saving per equipped vehicle × rollout fraction, less relevant incremental expenses. Use shipments/production consistently; consumer sales are not identical. General levers can cover ICE and hybrid vehicles, but actual platform rollout governs realization. Battery savings depend on pack architecture, size, chemistry and take rate. The current aggregate component assumption remains unchanged; this research strengthens the breadth of possible mechanisms without establishing a new numerical fleet-wide savings rate.

Next useful data: powertrain take rates for high-volume Ram/Jeep/Chrysler families and dates of architecture changes. Do not require complete BEV forecasts before evaluating common seats, electronics, body and supplier-standardization savings.
