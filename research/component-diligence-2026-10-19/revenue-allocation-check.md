# Price/mix proxy for the NA component allocation

19 October 2026. User proposed average MSRP comparison to replace the unverified 1.8x regional material-intensity assumption. A bounded search did not establish comparable sales-weighted regional MSRP data. Reported revenue per consolidated shipment is reproducible and avoids constructing a global trim-level retail-price dataset; it is not MSRP or pure vehicle ASP.

[2025 annual report](https://www.stellantis.com/content/dam/stellantis-corporate/investors/financial-reports/Stellantis-NV-20251231-Annual-Report-and-Form-20-F.pdf), PDF pp44,60–61,244,316; original 2025 segment vintage, not later restatements. NA 1.472m and global 5.484m consolidated shipments. NA revenue €60.962bn; global €153.508bn. Do not substitute combined shipments including unconsolidated JVs.

| Revenue basis | NA / rest RPU | NA / global RPU | Allocated portion of €88bn |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
|Consolidated revenue|1.795x|1.480x|€34.95bn|
|External revenue of six vehicle-reporting segments|1.934x|1.546x|€36.52bn|
|Vehicles and other goods revenue of those segments|1.949x|1.554x|€36.70bn|

Second basis sums segment external revenues €146.896bn, excluding Other activities and avoiding intersegment duplication. Third basis uses Note4 goods revenue: NA €59.674bn; total €146.167bn less Other activities €3.064bn = €143.103bn. Still includes other goods, not only new vehicles. Maserati remains separate from geographic NA segment. Regional and procurement boundaries are not fully reconciled.

Formula: m=(NA revenue/NA units)/(rest revenue/rest units); allocation=88×(NA units×m)/(NA units×m+rest units). Algebraically this equals 88×NA revenue/total revenue. Thus the critical assumption is equal direct-material purchase intensity per euro of selected revenue. This is NOT independent confirmation of true component costs.

Price differences capture equipment and mix, but also pricing power, incentives, sourcing, FX and margin differences. MSRP would additionally require volume weighting, consistent tax treatment, currencies and trim mix; a simple average of catalog starting prices would be misleading. A precise calculated price multiplier is not an exact cost multiplier.

Conclusion: revenue proxies support the €35bn order of magnitude; a narrower comparable revenue scope points nearer €36.5–36.7bn. Retain the €35bn working input and €30–40bn sensitivity pending decision. Do not silently replace the base. This adds a better documented calibration, not a new reported NA material expense.
