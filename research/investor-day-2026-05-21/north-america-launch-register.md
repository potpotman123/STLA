## Coverage notes

The register includes every named vehicle/family on D79 and additional propulsion/performance initiatives explicitly described in the North America remarks. The unnamed Dodge silhouette remains unidentified. Do not infer launch cancellations for current products omitted from the2030 map, including Voyager, Hornet or Wagoneer S, without separate evidence. No incremental US Fiat, Alfa Romeo or Maserati model is established by this American-brand map; global announcements do not automatically establish North American launches.

Wrangler Unlimited and Charger body styles are grouped to avoid artificial new-nameplate counts. The mapping between the illustrative showroom graphic and management's23-launch/11-new headline is not a verified one-to-one accounting identity.
