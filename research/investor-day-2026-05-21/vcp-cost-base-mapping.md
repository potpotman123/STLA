## Accounting perimeter

The four VCP workstreams are savings opportunities, not four exhaustive and mutually exclusive accounting expense lines. Do not force them plus AOI to equal revenue. Start with reported operating expenses and AOI adjustments; map addressable subsets into components, manufacturing, quality, and logistics/indirect costs. Keep SG&A and R&D separately visible. If an identified VCP indirect-cost project saves SG&A, recognize it there once and do not also add it as Industrial savings in the profit bridge.

## Four pools and outstanding definitions

| Pool | Initial denominator to seek | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Components | Eligible external material/component expense for vehicles recognized in the relevant year |75% disclosure concerns new-vehicle cost, not revenue or all group cost of revenues; annual purchases differ from cost recognized through inventory |
| Manufacturing | Addressable plant labor, energy, maintenance and other conversion costs | Define treatment of depreciation; keep procurement components, freight and quality costs out of duplicate pools; production quantity changes are not efficiency savings |
| Quality | Recurring warranty/recall expense plus separately identified quality costs not already in manufacturing | Avoid treating provisions stock or settlements as annual expense; keep scrap/rework in one pool; separate exceptional past-cohort remeasurement |
| Logistics and indirect costs | Freight/warehousing and explicitly eligible indirect procurement/overhead | May cross accounting functions; do not relabel all SG&A/R&D as logistics or indirect spend |
