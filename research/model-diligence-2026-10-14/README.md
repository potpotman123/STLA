# Stellantis model sales and pricing outlook through 2028

As of 14 October 2026. **The evidence supports selective volume recovery, with much less support for simultaneous recovery in pricing power.** Ram 1500 has regained a substantial selling pace. Durango, Wrangler and minivans need to sustain an existing franchise, rather than receive another large rebound assumption. Grand Cherokee and Compass require different explanations: the former had a better first half before its Q3 break, while Compass was already weak. Cherokee and ProMaster City can fill real product gaps, provided their delivered economics and availability work.

This package covers every requested model row through three detailed dossiers. The numerical scenarios cover **99.03% of 2025 US sales and 99.93% of latest 2026 YTD sales**. The remaining current sales are explicitly reconciled. This is the issuer’s US retail-plus-fleet reporting scope, excluding Maserati; its financial reporting scope must be reconciled separately. Unallocated future launches could be much more important than those historical percentages suggest. These are proposed research assumptions; the workbook and independent AI baseline have not changed.

## What actually changed in this pass

**Product availability now has stronger primary evidence.** Jeep says Grand Cherokee 4xe and Wrangler 4xe are no longer produced; Chrysler says the same for Pacifica PHEV. Dodge describes Hornet production as indefinitely paused. These current pages do not establish the exact production-end dates, nor prove that every current sale occurred after those dates. They do establish that old PHEV or Hornet peaks cannot be forecast as ordinary continuing-production recovery. Remaining stock and gasoline substitution must be considered. [Jeep status](https://www.jeep.com/4xe.html), [Pacifica status](https://www.chrysler.com/pacifica-hybrid.html), [Hornet status](https://www.dodge.com/hornet.html).

**There is tangible evidence for fresh Ram orders as well as clearance.** One dealer advertised financing terms that could be locked to factory orders, then separately advertised substantial stock discounts with different stacking rules. It also solicited MY2027 orders. This improves the evidence for desired-configuration ordering; it does not prove completed profitable national replenishment. Both fresh orders and aggressive discounting can be present at once. [March order support](https://www.ramforum.com/threads/stack-2-9-apr-w-rebates-mark-dodge-discount.223312/), [July stock offers](https://www.ramforum.com/threads/14k-21k-off-ram-1500s-mark-dodge-in-stock.226171/), [MY2027 order solicitation](https://www.ramforum.com/threads/2027-ram-1500s-open-mark-dodge.225859/).

**Affordability is changing through the product itself, not only coupons.** Durango's R/T 392 offers more engine performance and standard towing equipment. Pacifica separates some packages and incorporates Voyager as LX. These can make desired attributes easier to buy, but do not automatically improve manufacturer margin: extra content costs money, and a cheaper configuration can lower average realization. The Pacifica naming change also strengthens the case for a combined sales series. [Durango](https://www.dodge.com/news/durango-rt-392-hemi-returns.html), [Pacifica](https://www.chrysler.com/news/2027-chrysler-pacifica-debut.html).

**Returning gasoline is a correction, not restoration of the entire old Charger market.** The new R/T and Scat Pack have meaningful performance and utility, but the advertised entry price is much higher than the old inexpensive-V6 proposition. Independent driving evidence finds benefits alongside changed engine character. Gasoline growth partly replaces disappearing BEV sales: the combined family's 2026 YTD sales were still down 1.6%, despite the much stronger ICE headline. [Official prices and timing](https://www.dodge.com/news/2026-charger-scat-pack-power.html), [firsthand assessment](https://www.cars.com/articles/2026-dodge-charger-scat-pack-sixpack-review-crack-open-a-cold-one-gas-is-back-518451/), [official sales](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/stellantis-reports-q3-sales-302896446.html).

**Some launch details became firmer, while volumes remain uncertain.** City's August announcement establishes a $39,995 starting price including destination, improving on the earlier under-$40,000 target. Q1 2027 dealer arrival remains planned. Cherokee offers genuine hybrid utility, but management's July discussion of restricted trims/channels and future competitive trims remains consequential. A premium Trailhawk announcement does not resolve the affordable-entry economics. [City price announcement](https://www.ramtrucks.com/news/2027-promaster-city-pricing-announced.html), [Q2 transcript, pp12–14](https://www.stellantis.com/content/dam/stellantis-corporate/investors/events-and-presentations/other-documents/Stellantis-Q2-2026-Financial-Results-Call-Transcript.pdf).

## Where the incremental sales come from

The quantified base increases **114,854 units, or 8.8%, from our 2026 estimate to 2028**:

| Arithmetic grouping | Incremental annual units |
|---|---:|
| Existing families partially recovering | +49,012 |
| Defending recovered/stable franchises with modest net growth | +15,818 |
| Cherokee and ProMaster City ramps | +51,085 |
| Hornet and Dodge legacy runoff | −1,061 |
| **Total** | **+114,854** |

These are sales-row changes, not independent causal contributions. Cherokee alone adds 36,086 versus the 2026 estimate. The combined Grand Cherokee/Compass/Cherokee base rises to 345k in 2028 versus approximately 281.5k in the 2026 estimate and 330.7k in 2024. Therefore the forecast does require external recapture or segment growth; it cannot all be explained by moving existing Jeep customers to Cherokee.

Dakota, compact Ram, Ram SUV, Chrysler Airflow/Arrow/Arrow Cross and GLH remain unallocated. Wagoneer S and Recon also lack a defensible launch/restart selling curve. Any future addition must include offsets to the families above. If a new truck sells 10,000 units but half of those buyers would otherwise buy an existing Stellantis truck, the company gains 5,000—not 10,000. That is an illustration, not a selected 50% cannibalization estimate. No product's 2030 mature target is inserted into 2028.

## Dealer economics and pricing implications

The historical operator evidence remains useful, but is explicitly reused. Asbury described poorly matched inventory and low gross profit during Q4 2024 clearance, then improved ordering flexibility and rising Stellantis sales in H1 2025. Lithia was still critical in July. Those observations support a repair mechanism without establishing universal dealer recovery. The new Ram order evidence goes further by identifying a specific commercial process; actual completed reorder economics are still missing. [Preserved public-call observations](support/operator-evidence.json).

A dealer ordering framework uses anticipated sales plus desired ending stock, less stock on hand, incoming units and commitments. Faster replenishment is sustainable when sales occur at a viable contribution, not simply when production is available. An initial allocation, one clearance sale and a repeated fresh order are different observations. [NADA planner](https://www.nada.org/media/3077/download?inline=).

For the revenue build, the principal implication is **no automatic positive pricing layer on top of the volume recovery**. Entry-trim availability, gasoline replacing expensive PHEVs, affordable new models and retained financing support can all raise units while limiting average realization. Each family has a price/incentive/mix view in the input table. We do not have model-level manufacturer net-revenue-per-unit actuals, so no dollar RPU or precise price elasticity is invented.

Model-level customer MSRP, dealer transaction price, invoice and Stellantis net realization are different measures. A cash discount may be dealer-funded, OEM-funded or mixed. Promotional APR may substitute for a rebate rather than stack with it. Savings used to make the offer affordable cannot also all be retained as AOI gains. Unit weights already capture between-model mix; do not add a duplicate aggregate mix benefit.

## The few observations most likely to change the model

1. **Cherokee delivered entry trims and economics.** A dated H1 2027 rollout, actual customer payments, stocked configurations and repeated orders would distinguish a temporary cost/availability constraint from a weak offer. This matters for both its ramp and Compass/GC substitution.
2. **Ram's post-relaunch household pace and fresh-order economics.** Separate retail/fleet counts, completed customer orders, support retained by dealers and stock aging after comparison periods normalize. One dealer's offers establish a mechanism, not national magnitude.
3. **Grand Cherokee and Compass after the shutdown.** Actual available configurations plus customer sell-through distinguish production recovery from replenishing the wrong vehicles. Their different H1 histories make one shared explanation inadequate.
4. **Firm customer-arrival dates for 2028 launches.** Individual SOP, dealer arrival, prices and initial funded orders for Dakota/compact Ram/Chrysler crossovers determine whether meaningful 2028 units belong in the model.

If AlphaSense is used again, the highest-value request is one late-Q3/Q4 2026 US CDJR dealer principal or inventory manager interview discussing completed fresh Ram/Jeep orders, actual earned support and model-specific turn. For public dealer calls, Asbury Q2 2026 and its Q3 2026 call when published are the targeted next pair; group-wide GPU alone will not resolve the question. No broad transcript bundle is needed.
