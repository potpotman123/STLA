# Chrysler Dodge and other US model diligence

As of 14 October 2026. Provisional research views; US customer sales include retail and fleet and exclude Maserati. Annual forecasts are scenarios, not approved workbook inputs.

## Portfolio conclusion

Durango merits defense of a recovered franchise; minivans require durable customer utility and competitive payments. Charger has a gasoline recovery opportunity at a materially different price point. Hornet is a runoff case unless production restarts. New Chrysler crossovers and GLH remain visible but unallocated potential.

## Chrysler Pacifica / Voyager / Pacifica LX

Defend and modestly grow an existing franchise; no large depressed-base rebound. Keep one combined family so renaming cannot create units.

### Historical and causal assessment

2018 / 2019 Pacifica-family 118,322/97,705; 2023/24 combined 120,554/119,389; 2025 125,798. This was not a major 2024 lost-volume source. 2026 YTD 101,611 (+12.7% on regrouped comparison), Q3 34,491 (+6.4%). Old Grand Caravan footprint cannot be added to a contemporary Pacifica baseline. Voyager reporting transfer explains part of apparent Pacifica-only growth.

- True family recovery versus naming transfer: combined totals still grow, but standalone Pacifica growth exaggerates it.
- Price/utility versus reliability and running cost: Stow N Go is a reason to choose the product, while Sienna hybrid and retaining a used van are real alternatives.
- Fleet schedules versus household conquest: total sales cannot settle this without a split.

### Customer and dealer economics

Buyers need flexible passenger/cargo space and sometimes AWD. On applicable gasoline trims, folding seats save physical effort relative to removing bulky seats. A functioning paid-off van competes directly with a new-car payment. Owner discussion identifies repair concerns and purchase deferral, not an estimated failure rate. Sienna offers hybrid fuel economy without charging; Pacifica PHEV exit narrows the choice for buyers prioritizing operating costs.

A known minivan with differentiated utility can justify stocking ordinary family configurations; separable packages may reduce payment objections. However, brand-level high inventory and unknown retail/fleet split prevent calling the latest increase profitable replenishment. A fresh MY 2027 offer can require markdowns on MY 2026 stock. Dealer willingness should be tested with replenishment of the same popular configuration, net GPU and aging, not brand optimism.

### Corrective actions and opposing evidence

2027 refresh announced April 1 2026 with order opening and summer-arrival plan; factory/dealer-wide delivery breadth not established here. Voyager becomes Pacifica LX. PHEV is now explicitly no longer in production on official page. Refresh changes styling/content choice, not evidence of an all-new powertrain or solved long-term reliability.

Q2 41,704 shows stronger peaks are possible, but Q1 was only 25,416. Growing 2026 sales coexist with now-ended PHEV production; the undated status page and remaining stock cannot establish growth specifically after the exit. No matched dealer margin or household share series supports a sustained step to the 160k upside.

### Forecast and price implications

Q3 annual scale 137,964 and YTD annual scale 135,481 anchor roughly 140k. Base holds that level into 2028, rather than adding growth solely because of a refresh. Q4 base35k near Q3 and prior Q4 combined35,625. Down/up represent ownership-cost pressure versus broader conversion at acceptable offers.

Entry LX $41,495 plus $1,995 destination = $43,490 MSRP; this is a price anchor, not OEM RPU. Cheaper configurations and PHEV exit may lower family mix; reduced bundling can improve affordability without a nominal base-price cut. Competitive incentives remain in base case; no automatic discount withdrawal or positive net-price uplift.

Q4 2026 / annual 2027 / annual 2028 downside, base, upside units: [30000, 35000, 40000] / [125000, 140000, 155000] / [120000, 140000, 160000]. Null means unallocated, not zero.

2027 base140k unchanged; 2028 145k→140k. Removes an unsupported further5k while PHEV withdrawal/competitive response remain unresolved; not a measured5k causal loss.

Evidence that changes the view: Two normal-stock quarters above38–40k with stable incentives and genuine household growth would warrant upside. Persistent sub-30k quarters with aged stock despite refresh would undermine base.

Confidence: Medium on product/status mechanism; low to medium on numerical trajectory. Sources: CD01, CD02, CD03, CD04, CD14, CD15, CD16.

## Dodge Durango

A healthier existing business merits roughly holding the recent better pace, not another huge rebound to make up losses that have already reversed.

### Historical and causal assessment

2018 / 2019 65,947/67,599; 2023 69,196; 2024 59,357; 2025 81,168. YTD 2026 61,330 (+12.7%), Q3 22,755 (+13.7%). Recovery already exceeds pre-pandemic volume. Management cited Q1 2025 retail +64%, well before the 2026 392 change.

- Available differentiated V8/three-row utility versus generic rebound in SUV demand.
- More capability per dollar versus temporary inventory clearance.
- Retail improvement versus fleet volume: a dated retail statement exists, but current model fleet split does not.

### Customer and dealer economics

Compare Explorer ST and other performance three-row SUVs for family use, and Tahoe/Yukon where towing is central; size/cost differ, so do not call all direct equivalents. HEMI character plus seating and towing differentiates from generic crossovers. Better performance at a similar advertised trim starting point can retain enthusiasts; fuel, insurance and dated architecture remain tradeoffs.

Expanded performance content helps sales staff explain value and may broaden reorderable configurations. But giving more equipment for similar sticker dollars can transfer margin to buyers. Dealers holding old R/T inventory face obsolescence pressure; an improving new order proposition and weak used/old-stock realization can coexist.

### Corrective actions and opposing evidence

R/T392 orders opened March24 2026; Q3 sales release reports production milestone. This supports availability improvement, not a fully quantified national launch ramp. No separately proven full-generation 2028 sales date.

Platform age and improved competitors limit pricing power; current growth can reflect favorable fleet timing/support. The 392 cannot explain the preceding 2025 recovery.

### Forecast and price implications

Q3×4=91,020, versus YTD×4/3=81,773. Base90k assumes current quarter can be broadly sustained with new configuration availability; upside100/105k needs repeat evidence, downside75/70k loses the step-up. Q4 base24k lies between Q3 and Q4 2025 26,751.

Value improves through content and engine repositioning; do not equate unchanged advertised R/T price with unchanged production cost or net margin. Assume continued competitive support. Mix can favor V8 while per-content economics worsen; no extra aggregate mix uplift on top of model weights.

Q4 2026 / annual 2027 / annual 2028 downside, base, upside units: [20000, 24000, 27000] / [75000, 90000, 100000] / [70000, 90000, 105000]. Null means unallocated, not zero.

2027/28 base85k→90k, independently anchored to current selling pace and actual product-value changes; no extra derivative units added.

Evidence that changes the view: If normal availability plus content reset cannot maintain roughly20–23k/quarter without ever-deeper support, lower90k. Stable25k+ quarters with healthy dealer economics support upside.

Confidence: Medium on product/status mechanism; low to medium on numerical trajectory. Sources: CD05, CD06, CD14, CD15, CD16.

## Dodge Charger gasoline plus Daytona BEV

Modest recovery is plausible as the gasoline lineup fills out. A return to old combined Charger/Challenger volume is not supported by today’s price ladder or selling pace.

### Historical and causal assessment

Old Charger and Challenger together sold157,932 in 2019 and120,858 in 2023;61,810 in 2024. That is a departing two-nameplate customer footprint, not the new Charger target. 2025 Charger alone9,562 (ICE2,141+BEV7,421), excluding Challenger1,800. 2026 YTD ICE8,029+BEV767=8,796, down1.6%; Q3 combined3,679 is up22.1%.

- Replacement-gap supply loss versus rejection of electrified muscle positioning.
- Gasoline restores choice versus costly new vehicle still failing old entry buyers.
- ICE conquest versus substitution out of Daytona: huge ICE growth does not imply comparable net family growth.

### Customer and dealer economics

SIXPACK offers practical performance and AWD in two/four-door forms; it restores gasoline without restoring HEMI sound or old low-price V6 entry. A firsthand Cars.com drive finds real capability and comfort, alongside changed identity. Alternatives include Mustang, used Charger/Challenger and postponing purchase for a hoped-for V8. Do not assume all former enthusiasts reject the inline-six or return automatically.

Gasoline versions give dealers a more relevant product to order than a BEV-only proposition. Still, a $50k-plus entry and options approaching higher luxury prices require a credible payment. Mixed ICE/BEV inventory and uncertainty about future engines can age the wrong stock. The July 2026 R/T review reports a discount; it cannot establish profit or national program eligibility.

### Corrective actions and opposing evidence

SIXPACK orders began August 2025, deliveries announced late 2025/first half 2026; a July 2026 instrumented four-door R/T test demonstrates the entry derivative exists. Production/dealer breadth is not the same as mature demand. No street HEMI launch assumed in base; race cars and rumors do not qualify.

Q3 improvement and positive independent driving assessments support more than a zero-recovery case. But current combined sales remain small after gasoline availability, and YTD family is still down despite the ICE headline.

### Forecast and price implications

Q3×4=14,716. 2027 base20k requires5k/quarter (+36% versusQ3), 2028 base25k requires6.25k/quarter. These are explicit execution assumptions, not fitted elasticities. Downcase12/12k stagnates; up30/40k requires materially faster conversion and broader appealing variants.

Announced R/T delivered MSRP $51,990 two-door/$53,990 four-door; this is not former entry-level pricing. Growth likely needs continuing payment support and more base-engine/body mix, so forecast no pure premium-mix windfall. Falling BEV share may change average price and cost simultaneously.

Q4 2026 / annual 2027 / annual 2028 downside, base, upside units: [3000, 4500, 6500] / [12000, 20000, 30000] / [12000, 25000, 40000]. Null means unallocated, not zero.

Prior transition-family20k/30k becomes Charger-only20k/25k; runoff moved separate. The 2028 reduction removes an ungrounded doubling of recent annualized sales while retaining meaningful gasoline recovery.

Evidence that changes the view: Two quarters above6k with controlled incentives support higher volume. Persistent3k quarters after broad R/T availability, or order deferral for a different engine, undermine20k/25k.

Confidence: Medium on product/status mechanism; low to medium on numerical trajectory. Sources: CD07, CD08, CD09, CD14, CD15.

## Dodge Hornet

Treat existing Hornet as runoff, with any successor independently gated. Earlier excess inventory supports weak positioning before the pause; current collapse is not clean consumer-demand evidence.

### Historical and causal assessment

New launch grew9,336 in 2023 to20,559 in 2024 as its first full year matured; that was an offset to the group decline, not proof of durable appeal. Q4 2024 was nearly flat at4,993. Listing-based marketdays supply397.2 inJuly 2024 already signaled poor stock-flow balance. Sales then9,365 in 2025,802YTD 2026 and85in Q3.

- Performance-focused price/product positioning versus affordable utility competitors.
- Too much stock relative to purchases before production pause versus current lack of fresh production.
- Late launch year arithmetic versus lasting market-share gain.

### Customer and dealer economics

The original proposition emphasized power and AWD at a higher entry price than Trax/Envista, which offered accessible space and modern features. Those launches are useful mechanism comparisons, not identical cars or causal proof. Some enthusiasts value Hornet performance; mass compact-crossover buyers may value lower payments more.

Aged stock ties up cash and requires dealer/OEM concessions. An indefinite production pause removes a normal replenishment cycle even if clearance buyers find good deals. A sale of remaining inventory is not evidence of a sustainable fresh-order franchise.

### Corrective actions and opposing evidence

Official live page explicitly says production paused indefinitely. Exact start date and economic cause unresolved. May 2030 plan GLH is not a verified near-term restart or separate mature addition.

2024 growth and individual enthusiastic owners show the vehicle was not universally unwanted. Neither proves viable volume at normal margins. The pause could eventually reverse.

### Forecast and price implications

Q4 base100 close to latest85. 2027 base100 residual stock;2028 base0 explicitly assumes no production restart, not observed future zero. Upper cases allow a small stock tail only; a verified restart requires a new forecast.

Clearance incentives and low realization are appropriate risk; do not infer permanent OEM RPU from a fire-sale listing. Remove old-stock pricing from assumptions for an unpriced GLH successor.

Q4 2026 / annual 2027 / annual 2028 downside, base, upside units: [0, 100, 300] / [0, 100, 500] / [0, 0, 100]. Null means unallocated, not zero.

Previously unallocated; now explicit runoff supported by official production status. No automatic GLH units replace it.

Evidence that changes the view: New production/order bulletin with dealer deliveries would invalidate runoff treatment. Consumer clearance alone would not.

Confidence: Medium on product/status mechanism; low to medium on numerical trajectory. Sources: CD10, CD11, CD12, CD13, CD14, CD15.

## Other US brands Fiat and Alfa Romeo

Use a small stabilizing residual, not a turnaround needed to close the company forecast.

### Historical and causal assessment

- Aged/niche lineup and low consideration versus import/powertrain availability and price.
- Model exits/limited distribution versus universal dislike of the brands.

### Customer and dealer economics

Giulia/Stelvio are niche driving-oriented alternatives to premium German offerings; Fiat500e is a small EV proposition, not a substitute for the truck/SUV volume base. Quantified willingness to pay and US future-launch economics are not established here.

Low turnover raises the penalty for stocking many variants; a small franchise can rationally rely on orders and targeted inventory. No national profit estimate or replenishment assertion is justified from the few sales.

### Corrective actions and opposing evidence

No verified through 2028 US launch in this bounded pass earns a recovery allocation. This is a lack of a quantified contribution, not a declaration of no future products.

Low starting units allow large percentage rebounds from modest product changes; numerical uncertainty is high, financial materiality small.

### Forecast and price implications

LatestQ3 annualscale2,492 andYTDannualscale3,476 bracket a rounded3k base; scenarios broad relative to small footprint.

Promotional pressure likely remains a risk given sales trend; manufacturer netRPU and model-specific transaction series unknown. Do not treat low volume itself as proof of any exact discount.

## Chrysler Airflow Arrow Arrow Cross; legacy residual separate

Crossovers could matter materially, but no model-specific numeric launch forecast passes the evidence gate today. Keep disclosed potential visible alongside a separately quantified existing portfolio.

### Historical and causal assessment

300 fell29,213 in 2019 to13,169 in 2023,5,295in 2024 and574in 2025; current brand residual16YTD 2026. This is runoff, not a retained sedan franchise. Chrysler future crossovers are a distinct effort to re-enter customer segments.

- New segment conquest versus substitution from minivans and Jeep.
- A broad2030portfolio promise versus actual 2028 dealer arrival.

### Customer and dealer economics

Affordable crossovers could reach buyers who do not want a minivan or cannot afford larger Jeep products. Management’s projected positioning is not yet a delivered price/utility comparison. Airflow/Arrow/ArrowCross must be mapped to actual dimensions and prices before picking comparables.

Dealers need competitively landed vehicles, stocked practical configurations and dependable supply. Brand signage and an announcement alone do not establish viable turns; sibling offerings may compete for the same customer and dealer capital.

### Corrective actions and opposing evidence

Named in the May 2026 deck, broad2028expansion discussed, individual SOP / arrival and US prices unresolved. Preserve unallocated launch potential through 2028.

Failure to find precise dates is not evidence of failure. Shared-platform programs and dealer distribution may accelerate launch once economics are specified.

Affordable newcomers can increase units while lowering weighted vehicle realization. No assigned OEMRPU, margin or conquest percentage.

Q4 2026 / annual 2027 / annual 2028 downside, base, upside units: None / None / None. Null means unallocated, not zero.
