# Ram product diligence — 14 October 2026

**Investment view:** Ram 1500 has achieved a substantial recovery in customer sales. The stronger conclusion is that restored choice, improving availability and competitive purchase terms work together; the evidence does not establish a new growth slope or recovered pricing power. HD warrants a stable-level assumption rather than automatic normalization. ProMaster remains a useful commercial franchise, with no demonstrated reason to restore its 2023 peak. ProMaster City has a more concrete value proposition and price than the previous audit recognized, but still lacks verified customer orders and deliveries.

This supplement preserves the earlier research and proposes revised scenarios. It does not change a forecast workbook or baseline. All units below are **US customer sales, retail plus fleet**, not North American shipments. The accompanying JSON contains source IDs, history, calculations, scenario revisions and limitations.

## What is new, and what it changes

1. **A real dealer separates fresh ordering from stock clearance.** On March 5, 2026, Mark Dodge advertised qualified-buyer financing support that could combine with rebates and its discount and could be locked to a factory order. July 8 stock offers instead combined dealer and OEM discounts of $14,000–$21,000, explicitly excluding promotional APR and coupons. These are distinct programs, not a single package that can be stacked. This supplies a concrete mechanism for improved payments and desired-configuration ordering; it also prevents treating volume recovery as evidence incentives became unnecessary. One dealer's advertisements cannot establish national realized incentives or profit. [March order program](https://www.ramforum.com/threads/stack-2-9-apr-w-rebates-mark-dodge-discount.223312/), [July stock program](https://www.ramforum.com/threads/14k-21k-off-ram-1500s-mark-dodge-in-stock.226171/).

2. **Fresh MY2027 orders were solicited independently of old-stock clearance.** The dealer's June 18 solicitation initially specified no OEM rebates and described a $1,000 order deposit credited to the purchase. A June 22 reply preserves the original quoted dealer discounts; the September 10 edit removed advertised pricing, citing MAAP rules effective October 1. This is evidence of willingness to originate fresh customer orders, not completed profitable reorder cycles. A fall in visible advertised discounts after October could reflect advertising rules rather than improved transaction prices. This rule explanation is the dealer's account, not a verified national program document. [Dated order thread](https://www.ramforum.com/threads/2027-ram-1500s-open-mark-dodge.225859/).

3. **HD's refresh improves specific work tasks, not only the horsepower headline.** Cummins describes top-access oil/fuel filters, in-cab software updating, cold-start changes and integration with the new eight-speed transmission. These can improve service convenience and loaded driving. The supplier provides no measured fleet maintenance saving, achieved fuel saving or failure-rate improvement, so there is no numerical TCO benefit to capitalize into demand. [Cummins, January 7, 2025](https://investor.cummins.com/news/detail/664/cummins-launches-next-generation-6-7l-turbo-diesel-pickup).

4. **ProMaster City's price progressed from a target to an announcement.** The August 27 release specifies $39,995 including destination for Tradesman Cargo, $41,495 for SLT Cargo, and $42,695/$44,195 for the two passenger versions. The earlier audit retained only the March “under $40,000” target. Q1 2027 arrival remains planned. [Official pricing release](https://www.ramtrucks.com/news/2027-promaster-city-pricing-announced.html).

5. **Warranty terms narrow the ownership-cost thesis.** The MY2026 extension covers eligible original retail owners, excludes BEVs, is not transferred as the same ten-year benefit to subsequent owners, and does not cover lost use or revenue. It may reduce eligible repair exposure without improving reliability, downtime or residual value automatically. The contract must not be generalized to every fleet account. [Mopar terms, June 2025 revision, pages 1–2](https://www.mopar.com/content/dam/mopar/pdf/warranty/Ram_2026MY_10-100_Limited_Powertrain_Warranty-Jun17v2.pdf).

### LD: recover the proposition, then test repeatability

Combined Ram pickups rose from 536,980 in 2018 to 633,694 in 2019, then stood at 468,344 in 2022 and 444,926 in 2023. These combined LD/HD figures cannot calibrate a standalone LD peak. The portfolio included two 1500 generations; reliable national Classic-versus-newer-1500 annual counts remain absent. Earlier failed registration searches should stay closed. Canadian proportions cannot fill the US gap.

The 2024–25 story contains several mechanisms. Management described a simultaneous electrical, engine and vehicle transition, incomplete production ramp and missing desired upper trims. Classic's wind-down removed a separate familiar/value proposition, while its remaining stock continued selling. High advertised prices, available cash/APR support and customers' engine preferences all mattered. Pandemic chip shortages are not a demonstrated explanation for this later launch failure. Official LD sales improved from 187,013 in 2024 to 204,139 in 2025; early retail improvement preceded HEMI's H2 2025 return. That sequence rules out HEMI as the sole original cause or sole recovery mechanism. [Prior official series](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/fca-us-fourth-quarter-total-sales-increase-4-year-over-year-reports-full-year-2025-us-sales-results-302652833.html), [management launch interview](https://www.wardsauto.com/news/fixing-ram-brand-starts-at-the-top/799058/).

The current recovery is material. Q3 2026 LD total growth was approximately 73%, while 1500 retail grew 42%. Conditional on matching definitions, even assigning all 44,349 prior-year LD units to retail would leave roughly 13,674 of the 32,301 total increment unexplained by retail growth. This is a **conditional arithmetic bound**, sensitive to rounded percentages and Classic/family coverage, not measured fleet sales. It prevents calling all of the reported increment household conquest. [Q3 release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/stellantis-reports-q3-sales-302896446.html).

For customers, the relevant choices are full-size competitors, used pickups, a smaller truck or postponing replacement. A restored V8 serves identity and familiar-engine preference; Hurricane offers an alternative rather than proof every buyer rejected a six-cylinder. Entry trims improve the purchase ladder, but desired cab, drivetrain and equipment determine the actual payment. Eligible first-owner warranty protection can reduce repair anxiety while leaving inconvenience untouched. Selected owner discussions contain both happy Hurricane buyers and eTorque/service concerns; they identify hypotheses, not failure prevalence. [Buyer discussion](https://www.reddit.com/r/ram_trucks/comments/1umhbf3/price_drops_on_2026_1500_hemis/).

For dealers, a deposit-backed factory order can shorten speculative exposure and reduce wrong-trim risk. Terms locked at ordering can prevent a customer abandoning the deal if financing changes during the build. The new dealer evidence supports this process, but neither advertisements nor orders prove completed sales, margin or subsequent voluntary replenishment. A volume-oriented dealer can rationally accept a thin vehicle margin if its whole transaction works; that is not evidence all franchises have the same economics. Actual invoice, dealer payments and holding expense remain unknown.

**Forecast decision:** Q4 base 75,000 approximately preserves Q3's 76,650. Downside 65,000 is near Q2; upside 85,000 requires additional conversion. The 2027 base of 300,000 equals 25,000/month, slightly below Q3's 25,550/month. Keeping 300,000 in 2028 removes the prior unexplained growth increment and allows for smaller-pickup overlap. The chosen reductions of 10,000/20,000 versus prior are rounded scenario judgments, not a measured causal loss.

Base pricing requires continuing competitive support. Restored upper trims can improve mix, while entry/fleet expansion can reduce it; the net is unmeasured. Do not equate dealer discounts with an equal manufacturer revenue deduction. The base does not assume incentive disappearance or repeated positive same-configuration pricing. Falsification requires matched retail pace, configuration availability, fresh-order completion and stock/discount behavior after the relaunch comparison normalizes.

### HD: better functionality, no demonstrated volume acceleration

Official HD sales fell from 186,107 in 2024 to 169,920 in 2025. American Axle corroborated the early 2025 changeover disruption, yet every quarter remained below the prior year. Q1 retail and total trends diverged, so fleet/channel effects also matter. In 2026 Q1/Q2/Q3 were 38,597/43,497/41,107: YTD improved, but Q3 was still down. Neither an isolated launch shutdown nor universal household rejection fully explains this sequence.

Commercial operators value capability matched to their load, predictable maintenance and uptime; recreational towing households may weight ride and comfort differently. Cummins' service access and transmission changes address genuine tasks. A buyer with less demanding work may rationally prefer gasoline or a lighter truck to avoid unnecessary purchase and service cost. Peak pickup ratings differ from chassis-cab ratings; no 2500/3500 or 4500/5500 allocation is inferred from specifications.

Fleet intermediary Merchants listed MY2027 HD order banks open, July 20 production starts and 12–14 week lead times in its July 7 update. This supports orderability as a planning proposition, not actual production completion. Dealer stocking still depends on the precise body, axle, upfit and customer commitment. August's high-trim diesel clearance ad adds counterevidence to a simple premium-pricing-power thesis, but says nothing about national actual margin. [Fleet schedule](https://www.merchantsfleet.com/manufacturers-information/ram-2027/), [August HD offer and exclusions](https://www.ramforum.com/threads/21k-off-msrp-2026-instock-limited-longhorn-6-7ls-mark-dodge.226634/).

**Forecast decision:** HD's 2024 and 2025 Q4/Q3 ratios were about 1.128 and 1.162. Applying their mean to Q3 2026 produces approximately 47,064, rounded to a Q4 base of 47,000. Two disrupted years do not establish a reliable seasonal coefficient; the 41,000–52,000 range makes that uncertainty explicit. Base 170,000 in both 2027 and 2028 holds roughly current scale. The previous 175,000/180,000 assumed recovery that is not yet demonstrated. Upside requires actual fleet/retail growth and viable replenishment; capability improvements alone cannot quantify it.

Keep reported HD PU scope unchanged until chassis-cab inclusion is resolved. Do not add a second 4500/5500 volume line merely because the product exists. Effective business purchase terms and diesel/trim mix remain important, with no measured manufacturer RPU uplift.

### ProMaster: fleet productivity supports stability

Large-van sales were 46,600 in 2018 and 56,409 in 2019, reached 81,663 in 2023, then fell to 65,869 and 57,591. The existing evidence does not isolate fleet batch timing, availability or durable share gains behind the 2023 peak. This is an unresolved historical explanation, not permission to assume peak restoration. Current YTD sales remain slightly lower despite Q3 improvement.

ProMaster's low loadfloor and wide usable cargo space can reduce loading effort and repeated trips for appropriate routes. Those advantages matter alongside delivered/upfitted acquisition cost, maintenance, parking, driver availability and residual value. A lower roof/smaller van is preferable when large capacity is unused; full-size ProMaster remains useful when cubic capacity or upfit space is binding. Ford Transit, Mercedes Sprinter and used vans are practical competitors. Battery van economics require a suitable depot and route; no verified fleet operating-cost advantage is assigned here. [OEM fleet specifications](https://www.stellantisfleet.com/ramtrucks/promaster.html).

Dealers need bodybuilder schedules and credible replacements or contracts, not merely wholesale availability. Merchants lists ICE configurations and BEV orderability, but neither contracts nor delivery acceptance are disclosed. Upfit delays can leave an ordered chassis unable to earn revenue; third-party body cost also is not automatically Stellantis vehicle revenue. The commercial service relationship can drive repeat purchases, but a warranty is not insurance for a missed delivery route.

**Forecast decision:** Q4 base 15,000 is close to the YTD quarterly average, with 12,000–18,000 reflecting batch/availability uncertainty. A 60,000 annual base through 2028 preserves roughly 5,000/month. Remove the prior unsupported 2028 increase to 63,000. City can satisfy some smaller-route requirements, so additional City sales should not coexist with an unexamined large-van uplift. No new ProMaster generation is credited through 2028: the global large-van 2029 platform plan is not a verified North American delivery commitment.

### ProMaster City: price certainty improves, volume certainty does not

Old City sales around 13,000 in 2018–19 and 2023 collapsed to residual units after discontinuation. The one YTD 2026 unit is runoff/reporting residual, not the new model. The returning van is a different midsize offering, so the old series is only a scale check.

Its approximately 167 cubic feet, garage-compatible height and enclosed workspace offer an alternative to an oversized full-size van, used small van or pickup with a cap. The question is whether that task advantage is worth the new acquisition price after upfit, finance and downtime. Competitor exits could leave opportunity, but could also reflect unfavorable small-van economics. No absence-of-competition demand multiplier is justified.

Manufacturing in Bursa, shipment and Baltimore transformation create sequential delivery dependencies. The March plan calls for H2 2026 orders, 2026 production and Q1 2027 dealer arrivals. Merchants' April-vintage City row shows Q4 2026 production and expected Q1 2027 delivery, with the order-open field blank. Neither proves actual opening or start of production as of the cutoff. An initial commercial dealer allocation is different from repeat fleet orders.

**Forecast decision:** zero new-model Q4 2026 sales under the current timetable. The 2027 base remains a conditional 10,000, or 833/month averaged over the year. The 2028 base is revised to 15,000, or 1,250/month, rather than automatically doubling to 20,000. This is a scenario scale, not a fitted ramp; downside permits delayed/weak first-year conversion. Combined ProMaster plus City base sales are 75,000 in 2028, below large ProMaster's 2023 peak, but that comparison does not validate demand. Funded customer orders, completed conversion and dated dealer arrivals are the decisive missing observations.

### Dakota, compact pickup and Ram SUV

The verified May plan has North American platform SOP in 2028 for Dakota and the compact/Rampage-based pickup; no month, US launch price or mature US selling rate is established. Dakota addresses midsize utility buyers comparing Tacoma, Ranger, Colorado/Canyon and Gladiator. The compact offering addresses a smaller-footprint/lower-payment use case associated with Maverick. Both can attract new buyers and substitute for Ram 1500 or each other. Latin American sales cannot supply a US conversion coefficient.

The full-size Ram SUV on the 2030 showroom map is distinct from a range-extended pickup sharing historical Ramcharger naming. Its exact customer launch date is unverified. It could compete with Wagoneer/Grand Wagoneer, Durango and Grand Cherokee/L as well as external full-size SUVs. Leave all three rows unallocated; neither zero potential nor full-year 2028 mature sales is established. [Official North America plan](https://www.stellantis.com/content/dam/stellantis-corporate/investors/events/stellantis-investor-day-2026/presentation/Stellantis-Investor-Day-2026-FaSTLAne2030-North-America.pdf).

## Accounting, checks and stopping decision

For a dealer, evaluate selling proceeds minus acquisition cost, plus support retained by the dealer, minus concessions it funds and relevant holding/selling costs. A rebate already reflected in proceeds must not be added a second time. Promotional APR can substitute for cash support; the dated dealer programs show why eligibility and stacking cannot be guessed. No actual national dealer unit contribution has been established.

For Stellantis, MSRP, advertised customer price and financed payment are different from net manufacturer wholesale realization. No model actual RPU is claimed. Family mix changes should enter the regional mix calculation once; cost savings spent on affordability cannot also be retained fully as profit. The current evidence supports scenarios of continuing support, not an independently measured net-price recovery.

Reported Ram Q3 reconciles exactly: 76,650 + 41,107 + 16,314 + 1 = 134,072. YTD reconciles to 369,379. LD and HD sum to official pickup totals in 2024 and 2025. HEMI, Express, TRX, Rumble Bee and range-extender derivatives stay inside LD; no additive derivative uplift. North American wholesale shipments and the reported regional Ram shipment change remain separate from these US customer counts.

Discovery stopped after six targeted queries and selective concrete follow-ups across OEM, supplier, commercial intermediary, dealer and customer sources. Exact US generation allocations, national dealer margins, reliable engine-specific reliability rates and matched net transaction prices remain unavailable. Further broad searches would mostly add anecdotes. The highest-value next evidence is (1) matched LD retail/fleet volumes and manufacturer support after HEMI's anniversary, (2) completed fresh dealer orders and stock turn by configuration, and (3) City funded orders and actual production/conversion/delivery milestones.
