# Market, segment and company forecast revision

14 October 2026 · D097. This supersedes the fixed-level forecast assumptions in D096 and earlier audits. Annual US customer sales include retail and fleet. No workbook or independent AI baseline was changed.

## What changed in the method

For established families: next-year units = prior-year units × total-market growth factor × relative segment-growth factor × company-relative factor, rounded to 1,000 units (100 for the tiny Fiat/Alfa residual). Segment tilt is additional to the market factor; it is not the segment’s total growth. Company effects combine product, availability, competitive value, powertrain and internal substitution, avoiding multiple deductions for the same mechanism.

This is an indexed decomposition, not a claim to possess complete comparable segment denominators or measured future market shares. It makes the judgments visible; it does not turn them into independently validated coefficients. The annual2026 estimate remains the starting point and containsQ4 judgment. The same factor can reflect demand or ability to deliver, so causal attribution is not estimated.

Launch cases (Cherokee, City, Charger) retain separate conditional unit ramps; we do not multiply them by additional macro growth already implicit in those scenarios. Runoff and unallocated products retain their prior treatment. Heavy-duty and commercial vans use the mild market factor as a cycle proxy, not an assertion that they share the light-duty population.

## Evidence and competing interpretations

- EPA identifies a durable shift from sedans/wagons toward SUVs. Its regulatory truck-SUV category is not synonymous with pickups or full-size luxury SUVs. This does not establish a secular shift toward minivans. [EPA](https://www.epa.gov/automotive-trends/highlights-automotive-trends-report).
- Sienna sales rose from75,037 to101,486 in2025 (+35.2%). Kia’s official indexed release reports Carnival growth of44%, but full-page access failed; no full minivan market total is constructed. These gains can combine product launches, improved supply, fleet mix and household preference; they are not pure estimates of category demand. [Toyota](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/toyota-motor-north-america-reports-2025-us-sales-results-302652746.html), [Kia](https://www.kiamedia.com/us/en/media/pressreleases/24209/kia-america-postshighest-ever-annual-sales-in-company-history).
- Chrysler minivans grew only about5.4% in2025 using the combined family. Competitor hybrid strength means category expansion can coexist with Chrysler share pressure. Do not automatically transfer hybrid-market growth to a gasoline/PHEV portfolio.
- Cox’s September2026 update raises its industry forecast to16.1m from15.8m. The older January forecast is superseded. Monthly segment variation is not extrapolated through2028. [Cox](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cox-automotive-raises-full-year-new-vehicle-sales-forecast-september-sales-expected-to-increase-6-5-year-over-year-as-asian-brands-gain-share-302889501.html).

## Forward macro assumptions, not source forecasts

Assume US market growth of1% in both2027 and2028:16.261m and16.424m relative to Cox’s16.1m2026 forecast. This balances replacement/credit resilience against affordability constraints. It does not assume a return to17m, a specific rate-cut path or stronger fleet purchasing. There is no verified independent2027/28forecast in this pass. Flat or contracting-market cases remain plausible.

Minivans receive relative segment growth of4% then3%—approximately5.0% and4.0% total category growth after the market factor—well below recent rival growth. This is an explicit deceleration scenario, not a measured secular CAGR. Chrysler gets a1% relative company gain in2027 for retail entry/refreshed value, then a1% relative loss in2028 for hybrid competition/exit risk. The forecast can therefore grow while relative performance weakens.

## Why these directions differ

**ram_ld — Large pickups.** Availability/engine-choice recovery supports 2027 relative gains. In2028 maintain relative position; market growth adds units. No second HEMI uplift.

**ram_hd — Heavy-duty pickups.** Commercial replacement and refresh support stable relative position. Total light-vehicle growth is only a modest cycle proxy for this partly different population; no HD boom assumed.

**promaster — Large commercial vans.** Modest route/replacement growth; City may divert some marginal smaller-load buyers in2028. Upfit and fleet acceptance remain gates.

**grand_cherokee — Midsize family SUVs.** Limited2027 stabilization;2028 utility-category growth offset by hybrid competition, product/value concerns and internal Cherokee substitution. These effects share one company factor, not separate deductions.

**wrangler — Off-road SUVs.** Modest near-term retention/recovery; subsequent competition and powertrain-retention risk erode relative performance. No transfer of broad crossover growth to this specialist product.

**gladiator — Lifestyle midsize pickups.** Niche positioning limits benefit from crossover growth; competition/internal launch risk offsets some market expansion. Rounded flat volume is an outcome, not an input.

**compass — Compact/value crossovers.** Affordable crossover opportunity is favorable, but cheaper rivals, hybrid alternatives and returning Cherokee pressure its relative position. No unsupported broad restoration toH1pace.

**wagoneer_family — Full-size family SUVs.** Utility demand helps; early2026 strength is not fully extrapolated. Stable2028 relative position requires viable entry pricing. No badge-based premium-mix uplift.

**minivans — Minivans.** Recent segment expansion and family utility support above-market growth, decelerating sharply from2025 rival growth. Retail entry/refreshed offer helps2027; Sienna/Carnival hybrid competition andPHEVexit constrain relative2028 performance.

**durango — Performance family SUVs.** Utility and better performance content support2027; aging architecture, fuel-cost exposure and modern alternatives create2028 erosion. Recovery already achieved is not added twice.

**fiat_alfa — Small residual brands.** No verified mass-market value/hybrid repositioning sufficient to hold share. Small-footprint erosion; rounding to100units is arithmetic presentation, not high confidence.

## Worked minivan bridge

2026 base136,611 ×1.01 market ×1.04 segment ×1.01 company = 144,931, rounded145,000 for2027. Then145,000 ×1.01 ×1.03 ×0.99 = 149,335, rounded149,000 for2028.

These assumptions could be wrong in either direction. At the same market/segment growth, changing2028 company performance from−1% to−4% yields about145k; changing it to+2% yields about154k. Thus the149k point is a conditional case, not a finding established by Sienna’s historical growth.

## Pricing, channel and portfolio consistency

No automatic improvement in OEM net price is assumed. The model still needs competitive customer payments; savings used to fund that support cannot also be retained fully as margin. Growing lower-priced configurations may dilute family RPU. Model weights already capture between-family mix, so do not add another aggregate mix uplift. Fleet/retail splits remain unknown where undisclosed; favorable consumer trends do not prove total-channel growth.

Quantified portfolio bases now total 1,386,350 in2027 and 1,418,100 in2028. Future unallocated launches remain excluded, so this is not a complete US or NA forecast.

Prior downside/upside endpoints remain broad outcome stresses, not re-estimated macro scenarios or statistical intervals. Every new base remains inside those ranges. New-product ramps and conditional cannibalization are not added again. Grand Cherokee rounds flat because positive market/segment factors and negative relative performance offset; Gladiator also rounds flat for explicit reasons.

## Audit and next evidence

This pass used selective issuer, regulator and industry sources, not paid datasets or agents. Honda full2025 and complete Kia tables were not verified; no fabricated minivan segment shares are admitted.
