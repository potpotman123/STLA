# Selective North American launch check

As of 14 October 2026. Reuses the [May launch register](../investor-day-2026-05-21/north-america-launch-register.md) and the 14 October management-driver review. No workbook or forecast inputs changed. Structured records and exact arithmetic are in [launch-check.json](launch-check.json).

**Decision:** 2027 growth should principally be underwritten by existing families, Cherokee's continued ramp and the planned ProMaster City arrival. The dated evidence does not support mature Dakota, compact Ram or Chrysler crossover volumes in 2027–28. The latter require explicit launch-year scenarios.

## What this check adds

- **Cherokee Trailhawk is now a dated program:** the 30 September release opened orders, planned Toluca production later in 2026 and disclosed $44,625 including $1,995 destination. This is an additional trim inside Cherokee, not proof that the broader competitive-trim initiative promised for H1 2027 has arrived. The current Cherokee page advertises a $35,000 starting MSRP, but a website price does not establish stocked entry trims. [Trailhawk release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/2027-jeep-cherokee-trailhawk-off-road-prowess-adventure-and-efficiency-all-come-standard-302893832.html), [Cherokee page](https://www.jeep.com/cherokee.html).
- **The Cherokee constraint remains economics and availability:** July management described restricting trims/channels, applying VCP and introducing more competitive trims in H1 2027. It declined to date Belvidere production. Funnel interest was management's assertion. The Trailhawk announcement does not resolve that missing localization date. [Q2 call, PDF p13](https://www.stellantis.com/content/dam/stellantis-corporate/investors/events-and-presentations/other-documents/Stellantis-Q2-2026-Financial-Results-Call-Transcript.pdf).
- **ProMaster City has a firmer sequence than the garbled May remarks:** 2026 production, Q1 2027 dealer arrival and a targeted price below $40,000; assembled in Bursa, with Baltimore upfitting. These are plans from March, not newly verified completed milestones. [Official announcement](https://www.ramtrucks.com/news/2027-ram-promaster-city.html).
- **Recent derivatives are inside established families:** the October sales release confirms a Durango R/T 392 production milestone and plans Rumble Bee dealer arrival in Q4 2026. They do not independently establish full-generation launch dates. [October sales release](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/stellantis-reports-q3-sales-302896446.html).

## Numerical anchors, not forecasts

All figures below are **US total sales**, not North American consolidated shipments. Q3 multiplied by four is an arithmetic scale comparison, not a seasonally adjusted run rate, 2027 forecast or incremental contribution. [Stellantis Q3 model table](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/stellantis-reports-q3-sales-302896446.html).

| Family | Q3 2026 units | YTD units | Q3 × 4 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Cherokee HEV | 10,344 | 20,914 | 41,376 |
| Recon | 35 | 82 | 140 |
| Charger ICE + BEV | 3,679 | 8,796 | 14,716 |
| Durango | 22,755 | 61,330 | 91,020 |
| Pacifica | 34,491 | 101,611 | 137,964 |

Charger combined Q3 growth was **22.1%**, versus the ICE-only headline of 1,348%; combined YTD units fell **1.6%**. Cherokee, Compass and Grand Cherokee combined lost **23,588** Q3 units year on year; total Jeep lost **32,022**. These are arithmetic checks, not causal estimates of cannibalization. They disconfirm treating Cherokee's early growth as automatic net Jeep growth. The very small Recon count does not establish broad customer delivery capacity or a mature demand rate.

For an undated 2028 launch, the transparent structure is:

`launch-year gross units = mature annual units × months on sale / 12 × average ramp fraction`

An **illustrative** 60,000 mature annual units, six selling months and 50% average ramp yields only **15,000 launch-year units before substitution**. None of those three inputs is a Ram/Chrysler forecast or disclosed target. This demonstrates why a 2030 sales ambition cannot be inserted directly into 2028. No defensible product-specific volume estimate for the new pickups or Chrysler crossovers emerges from the available launch milestones alone.

## Provenance, limits and model gate

One bounded pass used three primary-source search families, then selective direct reads of the existing July call, October sales release, linked September Trailhawk announcement and official product pages. Search results for Dakota/Rampage largely concerned South America; they were not promoted into North American evidence. Direct linked Dodge press pages failed to open, so the October sales bulletin is the narrower source for its derivative production claim. No dealer scrape, order-book estimate or additional competing-brand census was attempted.

**Can enter evidence/model:** dated milestone flags and observed US sales; the sales must retain their geography, retail-plus-fleet scope and family definitions. **Cannot yet enter as observed inputs:** 2028 sales ramps, mature demand, capacity, cannibalization percentages, localization timing or net transaction prices. Failure to discover an updated date is not proof of cancellation. Next discriminating evidence is individual SOP/dealer-arrival dates and repeated retail deliveries at economic configurations, rather than another lineup announcement.
