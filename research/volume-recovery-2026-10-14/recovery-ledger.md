# Model-by-model recovery ledger

Cutoff 14 October 2026. Customer sales are US retail plus fleet, not NA wholesale. All annual/available quarterly observations, shares and vintage residuals are in the linked historical ledger; compact rows below pair the sales evidence with mechanisms and proposed inputs. Null forecasts are deliberately unallocated, not zero.

Numerical coverage reconciles to reported totals through an explicit residual. Causal coverage is weaker for commercial vans, small brands and some launch cohorts. The older unsplit pickup row must never be added to modern LD/HD totals.

## Ram light-duty pickups

**Observed scale:** 2025 204,139; YTD September 2026 202,147 units. 2025 US share 16.20%. 2023–24 loss not separately allocable.

**Earlier trajectory:** Pooled pickups weakened before 2024; US pre-2024 LD/HD allocation remains incomplete.

**Hypotheses:** Transition and trim mismatch; Classic affordability gap; Engine preference and competitive payments.

**Support / counterevidence:** Official LD growth and retail improvement; management documents launch/trim constraints. Recovery began before HEMI return; elevated brand inventory and incentives complicate attribution.

**Operational change and milestone:** HEMI and entry variants restored; sustained profitable replenishment unproved. Next: Sell-through after relaunch anniversary and repeated availability of desired trims.

**Pricing, overlap and inventory dependency:** Separate support costs, Classic migration and future smaller-pickup substitution. Sell-through and replenishment must support the family forecast; brand days supply is not model inventory.

**Confidence / falsifier:** High on sales; medium on mechanisms; low on causal shares. Retail share stalls despite normal trim availability, or stock builds as concessions rise.

**Proposed annual judgment:** base 2027 310,000, 2028 320,000; 2028 downside/upside 260,000/370,000. Q3 76,650 x4 = 306,600; YTD annualized 269,529. Base holds the better Q3 pace, then adds only 3% in 2028. Downside loses part of the ramp; upside needs sustained retail growth and mix availability. No separate HEMI or dealer-bonus additive uplift.
