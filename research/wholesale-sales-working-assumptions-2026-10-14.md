# Shipment/customer-sales ratio: working proposal

AI proposal, 14 October 2026. No workbook changed. These are assumptions, not reported ratios or management guidance.

## Base case

- US shipments/customer deliveries: **98% in 2027; 100% in 2028**.
- Canada and Mexico: **100% in both years**, retaining annual sales placeholders of 115,000 and 91,000 respectively.
- On current D098 gross US scenarios (1,354,950 / 1,430,700), this implies US shipments of 1,327,851 / 1,430,700 and NA shipments of 1,533,851 / 1,636,700.
- NA ratio is calculated as (US sales × US ratio + Canada sales + Mexico sales) / total customer sales: **98.264% in 2027; 100% in 2028**. Display 98.3% / 100.0%, but retain formula precision. Recalculate if the volume scenario changes or cannibalization is deducted.

## Mechanism and inventory check

2027 US receipts fall short of customer sales by 27,099 units; 2028 receipts match sales. This represents gradual clearance while retaining launch inventory. Asbury's April and July 2026 commentary supports replacement of expensive older stock with better-priced incoming stock, but does not identify a numerical ratio. See [Asbury evidence](asbury-inventory-direction-2026-10-14.md).

Stellantis reported June US dealer inventory of 390,000 and expected approximately 365,000 at July-end, describing that approximate level as supportive of upcoming sales and launches. [July 30 official call, printed page 14](https://www.stellantis.com/content/dam/stellantis-corporate/investors/events-and-presentations/other-documents/Stellantis-Q2-2026-Financial-Results-Call-Transcript.pdf). The 365,000 is management's expectation, not a verified July actual or year-end actual.

Use 365,000 only as an illustrative assumed opening-2027 stock anchor: inventory would finish 2027 at 337,901 and remain there in 2028. Starting instead at 340,000 or 390,000 gives 312,901 or 362,901. The drawdown is approximately 7.4% of the illustrative 365,000 stock, modest relative to the inventory base. There is no independently established optimal stock target. The 2% sales shortfall is analyst judgment; the arithmetic is a scale check, not validation.

## Implementation and limits

Use the US annual ratio in each quarter as a simple phasing convention; apply it to that quarter's sales, not one-quarter of the ratio. This does not forecast seasonal shutdowns or launch stocking. Canada/Mexico receipts match sales. Calculate regional quarterly ratios from the country mix; sum shipments and sales to derive annual ratios rather than averaging quarterly percentages.

Inventory identity requires matched receipts, deliveries and inventory scope. Treat regional shipments as a simplifying proxy for dealer receipts, with no forecast change in transit, direct-fleet timing or other scope differences. Do not silently force historical differences into inventory: reconcile those separately. These assumptions cannot recover actual missing historical inventory data.

Sensitivity: US 96% / 99% implies more sustained destocking; US 100% / 101% implies stable inventory then launch stocking. These are shipment sensitivities, not overall company bear/bull cases: inventory accumulation can reflect weak sell-through. Each percentage point of US ratio changes shipments by about 13,550 in 2027 and 14,307 in 2028. No probabilities assigned.

Revisit when year-end 2026 inventory is known, or if stock aging, dealer margins, sales or launch timing contradict the assumed clearance. A lower inventory/sales ratio in 2028 can come from rising sales with flat stock; continued destocking is unnecessary.
